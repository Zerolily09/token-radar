How the ranking works

A protocol with no token is worth, as a first estimate, what the market already pays for comparable protocols that have one. The snapshot carries those comparables itself: every traded protocol has a market cap and a TVL, so each category gets its own value multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of its traded members, shrunk toward the market-wide median when a category has few traded members to judge by. A pre-token protocol's base value is its TVL times its category's multiple. Three adjustments follow, each from the protocol's own record: momentum (its 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% and damped, so one hot week cannot dominate), maturity (the longer DefiLlama has tracked a protocol, the closer it earns the full multiple, up to three years), and breadth (each extra chain is another community a launch can drop to). No name is special-cased: one function scores every row, and every row's detail view shows its own arithmetic.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × (1 + clamped 7d change × 0.35) × (0.65 + 0.35 × min(age, 3y) / 3y) × (1 + 0.02 × extra chains, up to 7)

Coverage: every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist (pre-token, at least $50M TVL, not a wrapped or staked product) is ranked, and the radar keeps going — every other pre-token protocol at or above $10M TVL that is not an exchange, a versioned pool, or a wrapped, staked or bridged product. Protocols that already trade are never ranked; the Live view shows them at the market's own values, which is also where the multiples come from.
